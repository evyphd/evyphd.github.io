Research

Dr. Smith builds game-theoretic, mathematical, and empirical models of the AI content recommendation systems behind platforms such as TikTok, Instagram, and other consumer apps. Her work examines the design choices firms make when building these systems, including the hard case where little or no data yet exists on a customer or market, and how those choices shape outcomes for firms and consumers.

About

Evelyn O. Smith is an Assistant Professor of Marketing in the Gatton College of Business and Economics at the University of Kentucky. She earned her PhD in Marketing, with a specialization in game theory and minors in economics and statistics, from the University of Washington, and holds a BS in Business Administration from Saint Louis University.

Her research applies game-theoretic and empirical methods to AI content recommendation systems, the design choices firms make when building them, and the strategic problem of building effective AI when customer data does not yet exist. Before academia, she worked as an artificial intelligence product marketing researcher at Microsoft, and she has collaborated with industry and government organizations including Tata and the United Nations. She also advises Cheeki, Inc., a federal AI compliance company, as Chief Financial Officer.

She serves as a reviewer for leading journals including Marketing Science, Management Science, and the International Journal of Research in Marketing.
